It's not just you

  • Mental health in academia. Work organization and mental health problems in PhD students (Levecque et al., Research Policy, 2017): about one in three PhD students at risk of a common psychiatric disorder. Depression, anxiety and suicidal ideation among PhD students (Satinsky et al., Scientific Reports, 2021): a review of studies from many countries.
  • Why people leave. The rise of the temporary workforce (Milojević et al., PNAS, 2018): the typical career in science has shrunk to a few years. Gender and retention patterns among U.S. faculty (Spoon et al., Science Advances, 2023): most who leave are pushed by workplace climate, and women leave more at every career stage.
