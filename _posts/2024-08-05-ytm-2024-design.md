![The YTM 2024 banner: "YTM 2024 UNI-MÜNSTER" in pink next to the logo, on a teal background](/images/ytm-2024/ytm-2024-cover.jpeg)

From 5 to 9 August 2024, the [Young Topologists Meeting](https://www.uni-muenster.de/MathematicsMuenster/events/2024/YTM.shtml) came to the University of Münster. YTM is a conference run by and for early-career topologists: PhD students and postdocs give most of the talks, and two lecture series anchor the week. This year they were Ishan Levy (Copenhagen) on *Telescopic computations in algebraic K-theory* and Arunima Ray (MPI Bonn) on *From knots and links to 4-manifolds*.

I was on the organizing committee, together with Konrad Bals, Etienne Batelier, Edith Hübner, Marin Janssen, Alexander Kutzim, Lukas Stöveken, Maximilian Tönies and Thomas Tony. I also drew the logo, and Natalia Pacheco-Tallaj did the graphic design around it.

## The logo

![The YTM 2024 logo: the letters Y, T and M drawn as pink and cyan flames around a lightning bolt, inside a yellow scalloped seal](/images/ytm-2024/ytm-2024-logo.png){: style="max-width: 320px;"}

The letters Y, T and M are drawn as flames, hot pink with cyan shadows, and the T in the middle is split by a lightning bolt. They sit inside a yellow seal with a scalloped edge, somewhere between a sun and a sticker. Everything has a heavy black outline, so the logo reads both large on the banner and small in the corner of each day of the schedule.

## The schedule

![The YTM 2024 schedule: one lime-green card per day, with talk times, speakers and titles, smiley-face stickers, and a "Where things are" panel](/images/ytm-2024/ytm-2024-schedule.jpeg)

Natalia and I made the schedule together. Each day is its own lime-green card with the logo in the corner, speakers in cyan, and titles in black; the coffee breaks, lunches and the poster session ("with finger food!") get their own bands. Yellow smiley stickers and pink and cyan bursts are scattered across the page, and a last panel says where things are: talks in M1 and M2, coffee breaks and the poster session in the Cluster Lounge, and a silent room in the SRZ.
